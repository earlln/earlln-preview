Practices vs. guides: what's the difference?

Same topics, same content. Only the way you learn differs.

👆 Tap-along practice📖 Step-by-step guide
LayoutOne task per screenEverything on one page
PaceStep by step, checking "Did it work?"Read from top to bottom
When stuckHelp for that step, FAQs, ask Claude"If something goes wrong" table at the end
ExtrasSaves your progress, tailored to your computerAt-a-glance tables, easy to look things up
Best forTrying it for the first timeReviewing, or skimming first
