[Company notice] Changes to working hours in 2027

Hello, this is the HR team.
Some working rules change on January 1, 2027. Please read to the end, and if a change applies to you, apply before the deadline.

1. Wider flexible hours
Today you can start work any time between 8 and 10 a.m. From 2027 the window widens to between 7 and 11 a.m. The 8-hour workday stays the same. However, 2 to 4 p.m. becomes 'core time' for everyone: please be available for meetings and teamwork during those hours.

2. Working from home
You can work from home up to twice a week instead of once. To do so, message your team lead by 6 p.m. the day before and register it in the attendance system. New employees can work from home after their first 3 months.

3. Using leave
On top of half days (4 hours), you can take quarter days (2 hours). Unused leave can be used until March 31 of the following year and then expires. Please use any leave left over from 2026 by March 31, 2027.

4. For team leads
Please make sure your team's work-from-home days don't all fall on the same weekday. Submit next month's team work plan to HR by the last Friday of each month.

5. How and when to apply
If you want to change your start time, apply by December 15, 2026, using 'Change work pattern' in the attendance system. If you don't apply by then, your current start time stays.

6. Briefing session
There will be a briefing on Thursday, December 3, at 3 p.m. in the main meeting room on the 5th floor of headquarters. If you can't attend, watch the recording on the company board.
